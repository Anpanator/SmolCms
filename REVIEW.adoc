== Security

[cols="1,1,6",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Severity | Finding and resolution
| SEC-1 | High, fixed | Vertical privilege escalation in user management let MODs grant ADMIN or modify equal-or-higher accounts; role changes now forbid self-change, use `>=` guards, and recheck transactionally in the service (`fx-security`).
| SEC-2 | High, fixed | GD decoded unbounded image dimensions; 16,384-pixel-per-side and 20-megapixel limits are checked before decode at upload and worker boundaries.
| SEC-3 | Medium, fixed | Regular users could enumerate/read/tag queued images by ID; `ImageAccessPolicy` permits accepted images and `tagme`-tagged pending images to regular users, while MOD+ may access all.
| SEC-4 | Medium, fixed | Unbounded tag input reached `explode()`; `TagInputLimits` caps input at 2,048 characters, 20 tags, and 100 characters per tag, returning 400 on violations.
| SEC-5 | Medium, fixed | Demoted users retained stale session roles; protected requests now reload the role from the database and fail closed.
| SEC-6 | Medium, conditional, fixed | State-changing POSTs lacked same-origin checks; `SameOriginPreControllerAction` rejects non-same-origin `Sec-Fetch-Site`/`Origin` signals. Missing browser signals remain allowed for CLI/tests.
| SEC-7 | Low, fixed | Deleting one row could unlink a content-addressed file shared by other rows; unlink now checks references first.
| SEC-8 | Low, architectural, not fixed | SRI pins the entry script, not its ES-module import graph. Bundling is prohibited (no build step) and cross-browser full-graph pinning is unsupported; documented in `docs/security-considerations.adoc`, with the entry hash regenerated after refactoring.
|===

Pre-existing and unchanged: `cookie_secure=false` is acceptable for local development but production requires TLS termination and a `Secure` cookie. Login-form CSRF protection and username enumeration on login/register were not changed.
